Shrines :
    Shrines may use your dedicated mana bar, gained by fueling items into them.
    the shrine of evil is a special shrine as it is required for game progression, it doesn't spawn the same way.

    special :
        shrine of evil (start boss fight)
        shrine of resurrection (remove permanent health or use mana to resurrect a dead ally)

    items :
        shrine of mana (fuel items to gain mana, usually giving more mana than normal)
        shrine of enchanting (use mana, two item slots, the attributes of one will swap with the other)
        shrine of super enchanting (use mana, will scrap an item and give all its attributes to another item, can only be used once per shrine)
        shrine of reforge (use mana to reroll the attributes of an item)
        shrine of leveling (use mana for an item to level up)

    challenge :
        shrine of territory (locks player into a circular zone, spawning invincible enemies, surviving for a certain amount of time to win powerful items)
        shrine of vermins (spawn enemy for items)
        shrine of curse (remove permanent health for powerful items)
        shrine of the deep (for every shrine of the deep activated, the shrine of evil will have double credits to spawn bosses, but bosses item drop will also double)
        shrine of the abyss (will darken the area around in a 100 tile radius, all enemies will be elites, but bosses item drop will also double)
        shrine of the void (the shrine of evil boss will be elite, but bosses item drop will also double)

    helpful :
        shrine of vivality (use mana to create a healing aura zone)
        shrine of resort (use mana to create a safe zone for a certain amount of time)
        shrine of explosion (use mana to create a large explosion)
        shrine of teleportation (use mana to teleport away to a random location)
        shrine of mining (use mana to mine a trench deep down)
    
    allies :
        shrine of summoning (use mana to summon allies)
        shrine of transformation (captures the essence of nearby enemies, use the mana to transform into them)
        shrine of capture (use mana to convert all nearby non-boss enemies into allies)
